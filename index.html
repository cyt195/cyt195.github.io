Optimization Geometry and Structured Preconditioning

2026

Independent research · Sole author

  • Derived exact one-step progress and scale-invariant break-even compute thresholds for frozen structured preconditioners in a stochastic-quadratic model.
  • Developed Hessian-blind estimators using cross-fitted gradients and stochastic secants; evaluated 1,728 controlled paired-spectrum instances (Spearman correlation 0.993; log-price MAE 0.007).
  • Studied when progress gains justify the costs of richer geometry. The paper is accepted at NeurIPS 2026 OPT.

Decision-Relevant Scenario Trees under Planning Budgets

Aug 2026–Present

Independent research · Manuscript comments from Prof. Sean R. Sinclair

  • Studied representations of uncertainty that preserve Bellman action preferences and derived bounds connecting preference distortion to root decision regret.
  • Developed an exact leaf-budget dynamic program for pruning a fixed finite refinement hierarchy; exhaustively evaluated 57,286 candidate trees in a controlled three-stage inventory setting.

Manuscript submitted to Algorithmic Learning Theory (ALT 2027).

Physics-Consistent Self-Supervised Transformer for Fault Diagnosis

Nov 2025–Feb 2026

Team lead · First and corresponding author

  • Combined masked reconstruction, cross-modal contrastive learning, and temporal prediction for multi-sensor representations; achieved 76.21% Macro-F1 with 1% labeled data.
  • Used physics-consistency regularization for cross-device and cross-load generalization, achieving 81.12% mean Macro-F1.
  • Reduced embedded-CPU inference latency from 30.83 ms to 11.47 ms using token pruning and INT8 quantization.

Published in the PEED 2026 proceedings.

Multi-Vehicle, Multi-Trip Waste Collection Optimization

Sep 2025–Mar 2026

First author · Academic supervisor: Prof. Yi Yingying

  • Formulated a two-stage mixed-integer model with heterogeneous fleets, time windows, facility capacities, and emissions costs; implemented adaptive large neighborhood search (ALNS).
  • Evaluated a Nanjing case with 160 collection sites, 25 transfer stations, and five treatment facilities; obtained 26.0% lower costs and 30.5% lower emissions against the study baselines.

Manuscript submitted to Systems (JCR Q1, Social Sciences, Interdisciplinary).
